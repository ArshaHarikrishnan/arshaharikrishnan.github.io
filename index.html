ABOUT ME

Teaching Technology. Inspiring Innovation.

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, passionate about teaching, technology, research and student innovation.

My academic interests span areas of Computer Networks, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Internet technologies and emerging computing systems.

I believe that effective teaching goes beyond delivering content. My goal is to connect concepts with real-world applications and create learning experiences that encourage students to explore, experiment and innovate.
